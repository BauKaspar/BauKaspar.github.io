Collect winnings

If your pick wins, the loser's pool gets split among winners proportional to stake. Draws get full refunds.

The payout formula: When a winner is declared, the losing side's entire pool is distributed to everyone who bet on the winner, proportional to their stake. Example: you bet 500, the total winning side bets were 2000 — you get 25% of the loser pool on top of your 500 back. In a draw, everyone gets their full bet refunded.
